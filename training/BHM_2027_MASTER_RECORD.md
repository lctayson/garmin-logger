### Recovery conventions
- SUT/threshold: 2 min jog
- 15-min reps: 3 min jog
- Continuous SUT: no recovery
- 1K reps: 90 sec jog
- 2K/3K reps: 2:30 jog
- 4K reps: 3 min jog
- VO2 2–4 min reps: 2 min jog
- Strides: 60–90 sec walk, normally 75 sec
- Hills: walk/jog back, full recovery
- Easy: generally ≤150 bpm
- Recovery: generally ≤145 bpm
- SUT: ~160–166 bpm, cap ~168–169
- VO2: HR secondary; allow roughly 172 bpm
- 10K-specific work may progress toward 170–172 bpm

## 10. Scientific guardrails

- Most running stays easy.
- Two quality days per week are sufficient.
- SUT/threshold supports aerobic durability and HM performance.
- VO2/faster intervals are used selectively, especially in the 10K-development phase.
- Do not turn Garmin load-focus warnings into automatic prescriptions.
- Fast finishes are controlled durability work, not threshold sessions.
- The first phase is 10K development with 5K/10K calibration.
- The second phase converts fitness toward HM durability.
- Taper reduces volume while preserving race-specific rhythm.
