## What makes a good analysis here

- Specific to the data in front of you -- reference actual numbers
  (drift %, HR delta, compliance score, readiness factors), not generic
  encouragement.
- Say when something is unremarkable/as-expected, not just when something
  stands out -- false alarms erode trust as much as missed real signals.
- Connect to the current phase/goal when it's actually relevant; don't
  force a BHM 2027 reference into every single easy run.
- 2-4 sentences. This runs daily -- it should be worth reading each time,
  not a template with numbers swapped in.
